CLEAR, PERSONAL COMMUNICATION

Privacy & enquiry terms

This page describes how this catalogue works. Please contact Rumaira Corp with questions before sharing information or proceeding with an order.

Information you choose to share

The forms prepare a message containing your name, email address where requested, destination, selected designs, quantities and message. The website does not send that message automatically. You review and send it using WhatsApp or your email application. Those providers process information under their own policies.

Storage on your device

This website stores your enquiry bag in your browser’s local storage so it can remain available when you return. No advertising or analytics scripts are included. You can clear the bag by removing its items or by clearing site data in your browser. A website host may process standard request logs under its own policy.

Your conversations

Rumaira Corp uses information you send to respond to your enquiry and discuss an order. Please do not send passwords, card information or unnecessary sensitive information. Contact rumairacorp@gmail.com to ask about your information or request deletion of enquiry messages, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations.

Catalogue and quotations

Products labelled “design concept” are sample illustrations, not photographs or confirmed stock. An enquiry is not an order, payment or acceptance. Actual materials, dimensions, availability, price, minimum quantity, customisation and packaging must be confirmed directly.

Payment, delivery and returns

No online payment is collected by this website. Before proceeding, both parties should confirm the final specification, price, payment terms, production and delivery estimates, shipping charges, applicable destination costs, and cancellation or return arrangements in writing. Nothing on this page removes rights that apply under relevant law.
