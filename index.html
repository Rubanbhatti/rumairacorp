A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Add designs to your quote, set quantities, and send your request on WhatsApp or by email. We confirm the details and quote personally. Sending an enquiry does not place or pay for an order.

Can I request different colours or packaging?

Yes, you can enquire about custom colours, materials and packaging. Feasibility and pricing depend on the design and quantity and will be confirmed with you.

Do you accept wholesale or international enquiries?

Yes. We accept bulk and multi-piece orders and ship worldwide. Share your quantities and destination so Ruban can confirm shipping costs, delivery timing and your order details.

What are your prices and minimum quantities?

We quote individually based on the design, materials, quantity and customisation. Ask us for current pricing and any minimum quantity before ordering.

What about delivery, returns or damaged items?

Delivery estimates, payment and return terms are confirmed in writing before your order. If an item arrives damaged, contact us promptly with your order details and photographs so we can review it with you.
